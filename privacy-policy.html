Clear about your data

Privacy, with clarity.

This notice describes the EDGE website as it currently exists and what to expect from its connected services. We’ll update it as the site adds working accounts, email signup, analytics, or other features.

LAST UPDATED / OCTOBER 18, 2026

IN THIS POLICY Current site status Information handled External services Storage and retention Your choices Contact EDGE

01 / RIGHT NOW

Current site status

The EDGE pages are currently a static website preview, and authentication is not active until EDGE connects a Supabase project. Until then, the Sign Up and Sign In forms do not send or save the details entered. The newsletter form is disabled, and EDGE does not currently operate a mailing list or reader-account database through these pages.

The site also does not currently include analytics, advertising, or social-media tracking scripts. If those features are added, this policy will be updated before they are used.

02 / INFORMATION

Information handled by the site

Details you type into forms. At present, the newsletter, Sign Up, and Sign In forms are not connected to a service. Information typed into them is not submitted by the page. Please don’t enter a real password into a preview form. When authentication is activated, the sign-up form will send the email address, password, and any name or optional newsletter preference you provide to Supabase Auth to create and manage your reader account. EDGE will not send newsletter messages unless a separate email service is connected.

Basic technical requests. When EDGE is published on a hosting provider, that provider may process technical data such as an IP address, browser and device details, requested page, and request time in server or security logs. If you use the header search on a published version, the search words are included in the web request and may appear in server logs; don’t search for or submit private information. The hosting provider and log-retention period will be identified here once hosting is selected.

Messages to EDGE. If you contact EDGE through Instagram, Instagram processes that conversation under its own policies. Please don’t send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information in a social-media message.

If newsletter signup or reader accounts are enabled later, EDGE will explain what information is required, why it is used, which service stores it, and how to change or remove it.

03 / OUTSIDE SERVICES

Fonts and external links

EDGE loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files from Google, which may receive technical connection information as described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Links to YouTube and Instagram take you to services operated by Google and Meta. Once you visit those services, their privacy terms and settings apply. EDGE does not embed a YouTube player on these pages at this time.

If EDGE activates reader accounts, account details will be processed by Supabase Auth under Supabase’s Privacy Notice.

  • Instagram Privacy Policy

04 / BROWSER STORAGE

Cookies and similar technology

The current EDGE site files do not set analytics or advertising cookies. If account services are activated, the browser will store an authentication session so you can stay signed in; signing out removes that session. A hosting provider or an external service may use its own technical storage when you visit it. If EDGE adds analytics or embedded media, this policy and any required choices will be updated.

05 / KEEPING DATA SAFE

Storage, retention, and security

Because the current preview does not collect form submissions into an EDGE account or mailing list, EDGE has no such submission records to retain from these forms. Technical logs may be kept by the hosting provider for its operational and security purposes; the exact provider and schedule will be added once known.

When EDGE connects a service that stores personal information, we’ll describe the service, purpose, retention approach, and the safeguards in place. No method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06 / YOUR CHOICES

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may be able to ask for access to personal information, correct it, delete it, or withdraw permission for a particular use. If EDGE begins storing your information, contact us using the route below and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

Privacy rules vary by location. This notice is a practical description of the current site, not a statement that one set of terms covers every jurisdiction.

07 / YOUNGER READERS

Children’s privacy

The current preview forms are not connected to data collection. If account or email features are enabled, EDGE will set out its approach to younger visitors and any required parent or guardian involvement before collecting their information.

08 / UPDATES

Changes to this notice

We may revise this page when the site, services, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top shows when this notice was last updated. Material changes will be highlighted on the website where appropriate.

09 / GET IN TOUCH

Contact EDGE about privacy

EDGE is the brand behind this website. For a privacy question or request, message the official EDGE Instagram account. Please don’t send passwords or sensitive personal information through social media. A dedicated privacy email and the site operator’s formal details should be added before EDGE begins storing account or newsletter data.
